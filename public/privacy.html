Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Bottoms Up! is a free browser game at bottomsup.tech, run by Naman Khurpia. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have. There are no accounts, no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

What we collect

Together, this information can show that someone using a particular name visited from a particular city on a particular device. Only the site owner can see that combined view.

Your IP address

Your IP address reaches our server whenever you connect, as with any website. We use it to find your approximate location and create your visitor code, and we don't save the address itself in our game database. The lookup runs on our own server using a downloaded database, so your address isn't sent to a location service. The visitor code stays the same for as long as your IP address and browser details do, so it can recognize a returning visit.

What we don't collect

  • Your real name or email address, unless you choose to type or send them
  • Your exact GPS location, contacts or social media accounts
  • Payment details
  • Your microphone or camera

We don't use browser fingerprinting beyond the visitor code described above.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use cookies. The game saves a few things in your own browser so it works properly: your name, sound setting and best practice times, and your room seat so a refresh puts you back in your game. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What's public

Your chosen name, crew, level, time, points and race date can appear on the public leaderboard and Top races pages, so don't use a name you want to keep private. The analytics page is also public, but it only shows totals such as visitors, races and players online.

Sharing

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. The game runs on a server rented from Oracle Cloud, which stores the data on our behalf, and that server may be in a different country from you. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Daily visitor details (location, device, referrer, names and activity for that day): deleted after 90 days.
  • Race results and names on them: kept for the leaderboards until you ask us to remove them.
  • Visitor codes with their dates: kept to count all-time visitors.
  • Level statistics and page counts: kept as totals that don't identify anyone.

Your choices

You can play under any name. You can email us to ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, and we'll do our best to help. Depending on where you live, you may have further legal rights, including the right to object to how your data is used or to complain to a data protection authority. Because there are no accounts, please tell us the name you used and roughly when you played so we can find your records.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, and the detailed visitor page is protected by a private key. No online service can promise perfect security, so please don't type sensitive information as your name.

Children

The game is not designed to collect information about children. If you believe a child has entered personal details as a name, email us and we'll remove them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
